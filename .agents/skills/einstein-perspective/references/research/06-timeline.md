# Albert Einstein Timeline (1879-1955)

## Key Milestones

### Early Life and Education (1879-1901)
- **1879** (March 14): Born in Ulm, Germany, to Hermann Einstein and Pauline Koch
- **1880**: Family moves to Munich
- **1881**: Sister Maja born
- **1888**: Enters Luitpold Gymnasium in Munich
- **1894**: Family moves to Italy; Albert stays at Luitpold
- **1895**: Rejoins family in Pavia; enrolls at cantonal school in Aarau, Switzerland
- **1896**: Renounces German citizenship; enrolls at ETH Zurich
- **1900**: Graduates from ETH Zurich
- **1901**: Becomes Swiss citizen

### Miracle Year and Academic Career (1902-1915)
- **1902**: Employed at Swiss Patent Office, Bern
- **1903**: Marries Mileva Maric
- **1904**: Son Hans Albert born
- **1905** (Annus Mirabilis):
  - Publishes 4 groundbreaking papers: photoelectric effect, Brownian motion, special relativity, E=mc²
  - Obtains PhD from University of Zurich
- **1908**: Appointed Privatdozent in Bern
- **1909**: Associate Professor at University of Zurich
- **1910**: Son Eduard born
- **1911**: Full Professor at Karl-Ferdinand University, Prague
- **1912**: Returns to ETH Zurich as Professor
- **1914**: Appointed Director of Kaiser Wilhelm Physical Institute, Berlin; becomes German citizen
- **1915**: Publishes general relativity field equations

### International Recognition (1916-1932)
- **1916**: Publishes "Foundations of General Relativity"
- **1917**: Becomes Director of Kaiser-Wilhelm Institute; publishes cosmology paper with cosmological constant
- **1919**: Divorces Mileva; marries Elsa Einstein Lowenthal; Eddington confirms light bending prediction
- **1920**: Public attacks by anti-Semites on relativity
- **1921**: First visit to USA; awarded Nobel Prize (1921 prize awarded in 1922)
- **1922**: Visits Far East
- **1924**: Develops Bose-Einstein statistics
- **1927**: Begins debate with Niels Bohr at Solvay Conference
- **1932**: Appointed Professor at Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton

### American Years (1933-1955)
- **1933**: Emigrates to USA due to Nazi rise; settles in Princeton
- **1935**: EPR paradox paper with Podolsky and Rosen
- **1936**: Elsa dies
- **1939**: Signs letter to President Roosevelt warning of atomic bomb possibility
- **1940**: Becomes US citizen (retains Swiss citizenship)
- **1945**: Retires from Institute for Advanced Study
- **1952**: Offered Presidency of Israel, declines
- **1955** (April 18): Dies in Princeton, New Jersey
